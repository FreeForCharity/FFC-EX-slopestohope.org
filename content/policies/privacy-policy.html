1. Who We Are

Slopes to Hope is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 33-4379051) based in Breckenridge, Colorado. We recover clothing lost or left behind at hotels and resorts and distribute it to Coloradans experiencing homelessness and hardship.

This policy covers the website at https://slopestohope.org. You can reach us through our contact form.

2. The Short Version

This website is a static site. It has no user accounts, no login, no comment system, and no database. We do not run server-side code that stores information about you. The only information that leaves your browser goes to the third-party services listed in section 4, and to us when you choose to submit the contact form.

3. Information We Collect

Information you give us. If you submit our contact form, we receive what you type into it — typically your name, email address and message. We use it to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, ours records standard technical information through the analytics services below: pages visited, approximate location derived from IP address, referring site, and browser and device type. This is used in aggregate to understand which pages are useful.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

4. Third-Party Services

Because this site is static, most functionality is provided by external services. Each receives some data about your visit and operates under its own privacy policy:

  • HubSpot — powers our contact form and its associated visitor analytics. Privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics — aggregate traffic measurement. Privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on this site. Privacy policy.
  • Elementor accessibility widget — provides the on-page accessibility controls. Privacy policy.
  • RallyUp — displays our fundraising campaign progress. Privacy policy.
  • Instagram / Meta — embedded posts on some pages. Privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare — host and deliver this site, and process request logs to serve pages and mitigate abuse.

Donations are not processed on this website. Our “Donate” links take you to ColoradoGives, which handles payment information under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details.

5. Cookies

We do not set cookies of our own. The services above may set cookies or use similar storage in your browser. See our Cookie Policy for detail and for how to control them.

6. How Long We Keep Information

Contact form submissions are retained in HubSpot for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of our correspondence. Analytics data is retained according to each provider's default retention settings. You may ask us to delete your correspondence at any time.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it is used. Colorado residents have these rights under the Colorado Privacy Act. To make a request, use our contact form. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

Most browsers offer a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal. Where a service we use honours those signals, that preference is passed through.

8. Security

This site is served over HTTPS. Because it stores no data itself, the security of information you submit depends on the third-party services described above. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revision date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be noted on this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact form.
