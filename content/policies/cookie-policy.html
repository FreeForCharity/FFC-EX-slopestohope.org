1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make sites work, to remember preferences, and to report on how a site is used. Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser; persistent cookies remain until they expire or you remove them. Some services also use similar technologies such as localStorage and tracking pixels, which this policy treats the same way.
