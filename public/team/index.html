Drew Roberts

Founder & Executive Director | Board President

Drew Roberts is the Founder and Executive Director of Slopes to Hope. He established the organization after recognizing an opportunity to help people experiencing homelessness and hardship by recovering winter clothing left behind at Colorado hotels and ski resorts and redistributing it to people who need it most.

Originally from Wisconsin, where he owned and operated restaurants, Drew brought a strong background in business operations, organization, coordination, and team leadership when he relocated to Colorado in 2022—experience that has proven valuable in building and growing Slopes to Hope.

Since moving to Breckenridge, he has become deeply involved in community service and leadership, taking on a variety of roles including President of the Breckenridge Mountain Rotary Club, founder of the Tenmile Toastmasters Club in Frisco, and leadership positions within Toastmasters District 26.

Today, Drew dedicates his time and energy to leading and growing Slopes to Hope, building partnerships and expanding its impact across Colorado.

Erica Endorf

Secretary

Erica Endorf serves on the Board of Directors of Slopes to Hope, bringing more than two decades of hospitality experience and a genuine commitment to serving people, strengthening communities, and reducing waste.

As Regional Director of Operations for Sage Hospitality Group, Erica works closely with hotel leaders to strengthen performance, develop talent, and create memorable guest experiences. Her knowledge of hotel operations offers a natural connection to Slopes to Hope’s mission of partnering with hotels and resorts to give unclaimed items a new purpose.

Erica began her hospitality career as a reservationist at a beachfront hotel in South Florida and has since held progressive leadership roles with Sage Hospitality Group. Throughout her career, she has been known for her collaborative approach, optimistic outlook, and belief that the best results come from people working together around a shared purpose.

Erica is proud to support Slopes to Hope’s practical and compassionate work on behalf of Colorado communities and the environment. Outside of work, Erica treasures time with her husband and twin daughters and enjoys being outdoors whenever she can.

Kirk Barrell

Board Member

With an extensive forty-year tenure in the energy sector, Kirk A. Barrell stands as a seasoned industry veteran and geologist. Over the past thirty-one years, he has been a driving force behind the establishment of multiple enterprises, including Barrell Energy, Barrell Lithium, and Amelia Resources, where he serves as President. In 2021, he co-founded NuQuest Energy to focus on renewable energy project development across the Gulf Coast.

For the past 19 years, Kirk has served as President of Emmaus House Inc., a Harlem-based non-profit that has supported the community for six decades.

Kirk’s background in energy, renewable solutions, and sustainable development aligns with Slopes to Hope’s commitment to reducing waste and advancing a more circular approach to resource use. Drawing on decades of experience building organizations and leading nonprofit initiatives, he is passionate about supporting solutions that create lasting positive impacts for communities and the environment.

Helena Robinowitz

Board Member

Helena Robinowitz is a board member of Slopes to Hope. Since moving to Dillon in 2023, she has worked as the Sustainability Specialist at Arapahoe Basin Ski Area. Through her waste diversion work in the ski industry, she has become passionate in cultivating creative ways to reduce waste and perpetuate circular economic principles in the outdoor recreation industry.

She also runs the Arapahoe Basin Belonging and Wellness committees.

Outside of work, she is also a board member of Eagle Summit Wilderness Alliance, as she is passionate about protecting wilderness areas, and loves to trail run, snowboard, bike, and go to shows at the Dillon Amp.
